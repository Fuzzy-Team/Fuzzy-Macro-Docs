# Blender craft queue

The blender is set up on the **Collect** tab, in the **Blender** section.

1. Tick **Enable Task**.
2. In **Craft Queue**, set up each slot:
   * **Item**: what to craft.
   * **Amount**: how many to craft each time. Tick **Max** to craft the largest amount you can.
   * **Repeat**: how many times to craft this slot. Tick **∞** to repeat forever.
3. Click **+ Add Slot** for another item. Click **×** on a slot to remove it.

The macro crafts each slot in order, then starts over at slot 1. Empty slots and slots with a repeat of 0 are skipped.

To change the order, drag a slot by its number, or select the number and press Up or Down.

If the macro's idea of what is in the blender gets out of sync, click **Reset** next to **Reset Blender Timers**. It clears the macro's tracking data on blender items.
